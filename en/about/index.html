About Token Daily

Token Daily collects free quotas, limited-time offers and trial credits for LLM APIs, so developers can find usable credits in minutes instead of hours.

How we work

  • Check official docs and campaign pages daily for changes in quota, price and deadlines;
  • Label every entry: verified, partly verified, or unverified;
  • Record rate limits, validity windows and card requirements that are easy to miss.

What we do not do

We do not register accounts for you, sell quotas, or promise that any offer will stay live. Always confirm in the provider console.

Sources

Official documentation first. Commercial aggregators are used only as leads; nothing is listed until it is confirmed on an official page.
